# BRAND.md — Lumen Web Studio

## Who we are (one line, internal)
A small web studio for **solar installation companies** that builds fast, honest websites that turn visitors into leads and bookings.

## Positioning
- **Niche:** solar installation companies (local installers, not consumers).
- **Promise / outcome:** *"Websites that turn visitors into leads for your business."*
- **Proof of speed:** builds go **live in a week** — a differentiator, kept on purpose (see `docs/decisions/ADR-0003-headline-promise.md`).

## The voice
- **Plain English.** Short sentences, no marketing fluff, no agency buzzwords ("synergy", "elevate your brand", "cutting-edge").
- **Outcome-first.** Talk about the client's installs and enquiries, not our technology.
- **Confident but calm.** We say what we do and how fast; we don't shout.
- **Honest.** No invented client reviews. Proof is real demo builds until real clients exist.

## Tone cheat-sheet
| Say like this | Not like that |
|---------------|---------------|
| "solar companies turn local searches into booked installs" | "We build beautiful websites using modern tech" |
| "book a free call — no pressure, 30 minutes" | "Let us take your business to the next level" |
| "I do honest work that brings real results" | "we are industry-focused full-service growth partners" |

## What every piece of copy must do
1. Name the **niche** (solar installation companies) — the "who's it for?"
2. State the **outcome** (more enquiries / booked installs).
3. Lower the stakes of the next step — *free / no-pressure / 30 minutes*.
4. Point at the one action: **Book a free call.**
