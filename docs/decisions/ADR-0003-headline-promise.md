# ADR-0003 — Keep the "live in a week" headline promise

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context
The brief's headline formula (brief §2/§5) recommends a strong speed promise. The owner was asked whether to commit to "live in a week" or soften it for credibility in a niche B2B market (solar installers) where trust closes the call.

## Decision
**Keep the claim as written.** The Hero headline uses the "live in a week" framing:

> *Websites that turn local searches into booked installs — for solar companies, live in a week.*

## Rationale
- Fast delivery is rare among web agencies (typically weeks), making it a memorable differentiator when paired with the "free call" first step.
- The claim is qualified by the Process block (review the build before you go live), which keeps it honest.
- Trust stays high because: real demo builds as proof, no fake testimonials, and low-stakes CTA wording ("free", "no-pressure", "30 minutes").

## Consequences
- The speed promise must be *real* — the Process section must reflect a ~7-day delivery.
- Any later design that hides the speed line contradicts this ADR; change it explicitly, don't quietly drop it.

## Not sent: alternative phrasing on hold (if the owner later flips)
"live in about a week" (softer) — recorded here only as a fallback.
