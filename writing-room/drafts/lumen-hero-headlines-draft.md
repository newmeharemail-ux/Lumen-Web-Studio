## Recommended hero (option 1)

**Eyebrow:** For solar installation companies
**H1:** Stop losing $30K installs to a brochure website
**Subhead:** A conversion-focused solar website, live in a week. Not a template. Not a retainer. Just a site that turns visitors into booked installs.
**CTA:** [Book a free call] + Free 30-min call · No pressure · Live in a week
**Proof line:** Real builds, not mockups — see the Ecogreen demo

## Why this beats the current hero

The current headline ("Websites that turn visitors into booked installs") states the outcome but doesn't name the pain or the enemy. The new headline leads with the cost of inaction ($30K installs walking to competitors) and names the villain (brochure websites). It's visualizable — the reader sees the job walking away. It's falsifiable — either the website generates calls or it doesn't. And no competitor can run this line word-for-word because it's specific to solar's ticket size and the brochure-site problem.
